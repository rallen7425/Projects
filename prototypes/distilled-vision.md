# Distilled — Product Vision

**"All the signal. None of the noise."**

---

## The Problem We're Solving

People reach for their phone during dead moments — waiting for coffee, riding an elevator, winding down at night — and end up in an algorithmic scroll designed to keep them engaged, not informed. They put the phone down feeling worse and knowing nothing new of value.

The behavior isn't going away. But the thing filling it can be better.

Distilled replaces doomscrolling. Not by eliminating the reflex, but by giving it a destination worth going to — one that respects your time, respects your intelligence, and tells you when you're actually done.

---

## What Distilled Is

A **personal news operating system**. Not a feed. Not a briefing app. Not a news aggregator.

An operating system implies three things that distinguish Distilled from everything else:

1. **It knows you.** Your zones, your tracked stories, your reading rhythm — the app is configured to your life, not a generic audience.
2. **It synthesizes, not just aggregates.** AI reads everything and surfaces the signal. You don't read 14 headlines about the same story — you read one sentence that tells you what actually matters.
3. **It has a state.** You can be caught up. There's an end. The app tells you when you've seen everything worth seeing today.

---

## Five Use Modes

Distilled adapts to when and how you check it, not the other way around.

| Mode | Context | What Distilled delivers |
|---|---|---|
| **Quick Check** | Breakfast, coffee line, 5 minutes | 3–5 signal items, tracked story alerts, done |
| **Lunch Catch-up** | Mid-day, 15–20 minutes | Full today feed + zone status |
| **Mid-afternoon Alert** | Coworker mentioned something big | Tracked story surfaced immediately — no searching |
| **Evening Deep Dive** | Kids are in bed, personal time | Worth Your Time reads, zone depth, Make Me Smart |
| **Weekend Read** | Sunday morning coffee | Full briefing, long reads, background context |

The app doesn't change modes mechanically — it presents the right depth and the right amount of content at all times, so whatever mode you're in, the experience fits.

## Information Architecture

The home screen is a prioritized stack, top to bottom:

### 1. Tracking — Your Active Stories
Stories you're personally watching, often time-sensitive. A tracked story is specific, not categorical — "Celtics trade deadline" not "NBA news." Tracks may have an expiry (a deadline, a verdict, a launch date). When something significant happens on a tracked story, it surfaces here at the top, above everything else. This is how Distilled handles the mid-afternoon use case: you don't search for it, it finds you.

### 2. Today — The Day's Signal
AI-curated items from across your zones and beyond. These are not 14 articles about the same thing — they're the distinct stories worth knowing today, each synthesized to its essential point. Thumbnail-left format, zone pill for context, AI prose (not bullets).

### 3. Your Zones — Domain Status at a Glance
Zones are your ongoing domains: Sports, Local, Maine, Tech & AI, Finance. They're set at onboarding and represent your persistent interests — the areas you always care about. Zone cards are status panels, not filing cabinets. At a glance you know what's happening in each zone today without opening it.

### 4. More Today + Worth Your Time
When you have more time, more content waits. More Today extends the day's feed. Worth Your Time surfaces longer reads with estimated reading time — for the evening or weekend mode.

### 5. End State — "You're Caught Up"
A card that appears when you've seen everything worth seeing. This is not a soft landing page — it's the product promise made tangible. Distilled is the only news experience that tells you you're done. That's the design.

## Core Features

### Zones
Broad, ongoing domain coverage. Set during onboarding. Never expire. Examples: Sports, Local, Maine, Tech & AI, Finance. The zone card shows the current status of that domain — what's happening today, what's hot — without requiring you to open it.

### Tracking
Specific story or event you're personally following. Often time-bounded. Examples: "Patriots trade deadline — closes Tuesday 4pm" / "OpenAI product announcement" / "Celtics playoff series." Tracking is not a category filter — it's a story you've decided matters to you right now. When something significant happens, Distilled surfaces it. When the event passes, the track can expire.

### Read Later
A temporary reading queue — articles you've flagged as interesting but don't have time for right now. Not a permanent saved library, but a "get to this soon" list. Cleared as you read. Accessible from the nav.

### AI Story Snapshot
The core reading experience. Tap any story and instead of being sent to a single article, you get Distilled's AI-synthesized summary of the topic — a short, well-written paragraph or two that tells you what happened, why it matters, and what's still unresolved. Below the synthesis are links to the original sources (ESPN, AP, Boston Globe, etc.) for anyone who wants the full detail. The key insight: you don't have to choose between "read the headline" and "read five articles." Distilled gives you the synthesis by default, with full sources available one tap away. This is the feature that makes Distilled genuinely different from a news aggregator — the AI isn't just picking stories, it's writing the brief.

### Make Me Smart *(future)*
Contextual background cards that surface when you're reading a story. The war's been going on for 2 years — here's the 60-second version of how we got here. Microlearning tied to live news. Planned for a later phase.

## Design Principles

**Sophisticated, not busy.** The aesthetic is dark, clean, and AI-native — inspired by Perplexity, Linear, Vercel. Zone colors appear as thin accents, not fills. The UI steps back; the content steps forward.

**Visuals are signal, not decoration.** Thumbnails help you orient quickly. Zone images convey what's happening in that zone today. Images with no purpose don't exist.

**AI synthesis is the product.** The reason there are 5 items in Today instead of 50 is because an AI read the 50 and gave you the 5 that matter. That intelligence should be palpable — in the prose style, in the curation, in the absence of noise.

**Finite, not infinite.** Every design decision that implies "keep scrolling" is a failure mode. The app has an end. The end is the point.

**Built for your life, not a general audience.** Your zones, your tracked stories, your read later queue — Distilled is configured to who you are, not who the average user is.

---

## What Success Looks Like

A user picks up their phone during a dead moment. Opens Distilled. Within 60 seconds they know: what's happening in the world that matters to them, whether anything has broken on their tracked stories, and whether there's anything worth reading later tonight. They put the phone down and feel informed, not anxious. They did not open Twitter or Instagram during that window.

That's the product.
