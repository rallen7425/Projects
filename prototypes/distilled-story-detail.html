Celtics Select Alexandre Sarr with No. 2 Pick, Signaling a Rebuild Direction

Distilled AI

The Boston Celtics used the second overall pick in the 2024 NBA Draft to select French center Alexandre Sarr from Perth in the NBL. The selection surprised analysts who projected the team might trade down or target guard depth, and signals a willingness to build long-term youth alongside their championship core rather than chase an immediate win-now move.

At 7-foot-1 with a 7-foot-5 wingspan, Sarr is a modern, switchable big who fits the defensive scheme Brad Stevens has been building toward. He averaged 13.5 points and 7.3 rebounds in his lone NBL season and drew comparisons to Kristaps Porzingis for his two-way potential. He is not expected to start immediately. The Celtics now hold three first-round picks in the next two drafts.

The pick is viewed as a calculated bet that Sarr's ceiling is worth the short-term luxury of developing a second-year player while running championship contention with the existing core.
